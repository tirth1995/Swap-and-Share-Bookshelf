SWAP AND SHARE BOOKSHELF

"Swap and Share" books online typically refer to platforms where users can exchange, lend, or share books digitally. These sites often allow readers to upload books they no longer need or want, making them available for others to borrow or download for free or for a trade. It's an eco-friendly way to share literary resources without the need for physical copies. These platforms may host a wide range of genres, from educational and academic books to fiction and non-fiction. Examples include services like BookSwap, PaperBackSwap, or Goodreads' Book Exchange groups, where users can trade books with others in their community.
